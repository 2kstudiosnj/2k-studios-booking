About 2K Studios

Photography with style, elegance, and emotion

At 2K STUDIOS, every session is created to feel premium, personal, and memorable. Our goal is to turn your moments into polished, beautiful images you will love for years.

From birthdays and beauty portraits to weddings, maternity, graduation, family sessions, and product shoots, we help you look your best and preserve your story with intention.

We now serve two areas: our studio in Woodland Park, New Jersey, led by photographer Max — and Rochester, New York, led by photographer Dieunel, our growing home for portrait sessions in upstate New York.

We believe your memories deserve more than simple photos. They deserve artistry.
